Who we are

Built by players, run like ops.

The Rotten Republic started as a way to give Bangladesh's Honor of Kings community proper tournaments — real brackets, real prize pools, real accountability — without needing a big-brand organization behind it. This site is where that runs from: what's live, what's coming, and the receipts for every taka that moves.
